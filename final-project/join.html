Volunteer with us

You don’t need to be an expert. If you care about big cats and can give a few hours, we would love to have you.

What volunteers do

  • Help run stalls, fun runs and bake sales
  • Make posters, art and handouts
  • Give short talks at schools and the library
  • Help with this website and our social media

New volunteers start at our training session on January 30.
