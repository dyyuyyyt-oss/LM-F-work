Riverside Big Cat Guardians

We are a volunteer club in Riverside. We help people learn about the world’s wild big cats, and we raise money for the teams that protect them.

Volunteer with us Meet the big cats

What we do

Learn

Each month we study one big cat. Then we share what we found in short talks at schools and the library.

Share

We run market stalls, poster workshops and film nights, so more people in Riverside know why big cats matter.

Raise money

Bake sales and fun runs raise money that we give to wildlife conservation groups working in the field.
