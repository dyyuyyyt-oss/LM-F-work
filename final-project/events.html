Events

Everyone is welcome at our events, and most of them are free. Come along, learn something new, and meet the club.

Big cat awareness days

We plan many of our events around these awareness days, which happen on the same dates every year.

Good to know

  • Children under 12 should come with a parent or another adult they know.
  • All of our event places have step-free access and accessible restrooms.
  • Every penny from donations and sales goes to big cat conservation.
  • Want to help run an event? Volunteer with Riverside Big Cat Guardians.

Riverside Big Cat Guardians

A volunteer club that helps people in Riverside learn about wild big cats and raises money for the people who protect them.

This is a made-up club, created for a school web design project.
